    How to read this, and what to check first

    Each row is one capability cell from the Matrix sheet of ACE_Capability_Matrix.xlsx: a language, a content type and a tier, with one status for full text and one for index.

    The status scale runs from Out of scope and Not assessed, through Gap, Planning, In development and Pilot, to Production (partial), Production (at scale) and Mature. In the summary, "in production" means Production (partial) or higher.

    A language with no coloured cells has no row in the workbook yet.

    Check first: tier is still TBD for census, newspapers, books, cards and marriage packets. Rows for the Q4 2026 runs reflect October plans, not the 7 November gate or the Puebla Run 1 audit. Estimated images on the rows come from pages of different dates and don't reconcile.

    The unindexed-image counts by language come from the ace_matrix_feed.sql query run in Databricks, not from the workbook. A group with any top-tier record type counts as top-tier. Mid-tier means mid-tier record types and no top-tier ones. Estimated images on the rows are older planning figures that the query does not change.

    The 2027 list holds — record sets across — countries and — languages. For — of them, Databricks counts — unindexed images in — natural groups, against — groups on the list. — of those images are High priority, — Medium and — Low.

    The list comes from Content_To_Key_Sep_2026.xlsx and the counts from the country query run in Databricks. The workbook lists countries, so each country is counted under one language, listed in the file data/country_languages.json. Poland and Switzerland also hold records in other languages.
